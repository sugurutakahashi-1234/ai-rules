# Search and AI Visibility by Content Type

Reader-first guidance for common page types. Platform effects are not assumed; verify access and measure discovery, citation, referral, and user action separately.

For the cross-cutting strategy, see [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md).

---

## SaaS Product Pages

**Goal:** Help a buyer understand the product, its fit, limits, and terms. Observe citation and recommendation separately; neither is guaranteed.

**Optimize:**

- Clear product description in first paragraph (what it does, who it's for)
- Feature comparison tables (you vs. category, not just competitors)
- Specific metrics ("processes 10,000 transactions/sec" not "blazing fast")
- Customer count or social proof with numbers
- Decision-critical pricing, limits, and terms in accessible semantic HTML when the business can publish them
- FAQ section addressing common buyer questions

---

## Blog Content

**Goal:** Add first-hand, verifiable knowledge that helps the intended reader complete a task.

**Optimize:**

- One clear reader task per post, informed by the site's query map
- Definition in first paragraph for "What is" queries
- Original data, research, or expert quotes
- "Last updated" date visible
- Author bio with relevant credentials
- Internal links to related product/feature pages

---

## Comparison / Alternative Pages

**Goal:** Help the reader make a defensible choice using explicit, current criteria.

**Optimize:**

- Structured comparison tables (not just prose)
- Fair and balanced, with criteria and evidence the reader can inspect
- Specific criteria with ratings or scores
- Updated pricing and feature data
- Link to current primary product sources for claims about alternatives

---

## Documentation / Help Content

**Goal:** Let the reader reproduce a result and diagnose likely failure modes.

**Optimize:**

- Step-by-step format with numbered lists
- Code examples where relevant
- Structured data only when it matches visible content and a current documented consumer
- Screenshots with descriptive alt text
- Clear prerequisites and expected outcomes

---

## Local Business / Ecom (Google emphasis)

Google says Merchant Center feeds and Google Business Profiles can help eligible product, service, and local details appear in Search, including generative AI features. Check current eligibility and availability:

- **Merchant Center feeds** kept current with accurate inventory, pricing, attributes
- **Google Business Profile** complete with hours, services, photos, posts, Q&A answered
- **Reviews** — genuine reviews and policy-compliant responses
- **Structured data** that matches visible details and a currently supported Google feature
- **Business Agent** (where available) for conversational customer engagement
