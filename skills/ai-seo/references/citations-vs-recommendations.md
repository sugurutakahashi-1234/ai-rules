# Citations and Recommendations Are Different Observations

Do not collapse every AI-search outcome into one "visibility" score. A page can be discovered without being cited, cited without the brand being mentioned, or mentioned without being recommended.

This ladder is measurement vocabulary, not a claim that every platform uses separate systems or the same causal factors.

| Observation      | What can be confirmed                                               | Useful evidence                                     |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Discoverable** | A canonical page is indexable and available to the relevant crawler | crawler/index reports, logs, URL inspection         |
| **Shown**        | The page appears in a search or answer experience                   | first-party console, dated query sample             |
| **Cited**        | The page is displayed as a source                                   | cited URL, Bing AI Performance, dated prompt sample |
| **Mentioned**    | The entity is named in the answer                                   | dated prompt sample with wording                    |
| **Recommended**  | The entity is explicitly proposed for the user's requirements       | dated prompt sample with requirements and wording   |
| **Visited**      | A user reached the site                                             | referral and analytics data                         |
| **Acted upon**   | The user completed the site's intended action                       | site or business outcome data                       |

## Interpretation Rules

- A citation count does not prove ranking, authority, importance, sentiment, or recommendation.
- A recommendation sample does not prove stable platform behavior. Results can vary by date, locale, model, account state, and query wording.
- Branded search growth is not proof of AI influence without stronger attribution.
- Third-party vendor studies can generate hypotheses, but their percentages need a direct source, population, collection period, and limitations before reuse.
- Reviews, forums, press, videos, and analyst material may provide independent context. Do not manufacture participation or claim a universal weighting.

## Buyer-Guide Content

Self-authored comparison or "best" pages can be useful when they give readers honest criteria and current evidence. They can also be cited for facts about competitors without causing the publishing brand to be recommended.

Before publishing:

1. Define whether success means traffic, category education, citation, shortlist inclusion, or conversion.
2. Disclose the publisher's relationship to the compared products.
3. Link every material price, capability, and limitation to a current source.
4. Avoid rankings that cannot be reproduced from explicit criteria.
5. Measure citations and recommendations separately.

## Reporting

Report a small set of direct observations:

- first-party search impressions and cited pages
- representative query or prompt samples with collection conditions
- referrals from known AI surfaces
- the user's downstream action
- unknown or unavailable measurements

Do not estimate invisible retrieval as a fact, combine these observations into an unexplained score, or promise that increasing one rung will cause the next.
